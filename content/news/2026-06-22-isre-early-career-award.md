Recently, Researcher Fang Xia, affiliated with the Department of Psychology and
Behavioral Sciences at Zhejiang University and the Key Laboratory of Brain Development,
Mental Health, and Well-being of Zhejiang Province, received the 2026 “Early Career
Award” from the International Society for Research on Emotion (ISRE). This award is
established to recognize early-career scholars who have made outstanding academic
achievements and demonstrated significant potential for future development in the field
of emotion science. This year, only two scholars worldwide were honored with this award.

According to the official announcement published on the ISRE website, Researcher Fang
Xia received this honor in recognition of her innovative and high-quality research
program, distinguished by a distinctive cross-cultural perspective and substantial
empirical contributions that have consistently advanced the field of emotion science.

![ISRE Early Career Award announcement naming Katie Hoemann and Xia Fang as winners, with an honorable mention for Eva Pool](/assets/img/news/isre-early-career.png){.news-img-center width="560"}

It is reported that Researcher Fang Xia has been invited to deliver an award lecture at
the 2026 ISRE Annual Conference and to contribute an invited paper to Emotion Review, a
leading international journal in the field of emotion science.

## About the International Society for Research on Emotion (ISRE)

Founded in 1984, the International Society for Research on Emotion is a highly
influential academic organization in the field of emotion research, long dedicated to
promoting theoretical development, methodological innovation, and interdisciplinary
exchange within the field. The society brings together researchers from diverse
disciplines, including psychology, neuroscience, philosophy, linguistics, medicine,
computer science, management, sociology, and anthropology. ISRE organizes an
international conference biennially, showcasing cutting-edge advances in global emotion
research, and launched its flagship journal, Emotion Review, in 2009, providing an
important platform for international scholarly communication in emotion science.

## Faculty Profile

Dr. Fang Xia is a “Hundred Talents Program” Researcher and Doctoral Supervisor at the
Department of Psychology and Behavioral Sciences, Zhejiang University. She is a
certified expert in the Facial Action Coding System (FACS) and serves as the Principal
Investigator of the Emotion and Culture Lab. Dr. Fang’s research focuses on emotion and
cultural psychology, with particular interest in how individuals express and perceive
emotions through bodily signals such as facial expressions and voice, as well as through
artistic forms including music and painting. She systematically examines, from a
cross-cultural perspective, how cultural factors shape emotional communication and
social cognitive processes. Her relevant findings have been published as first-or
corresponding-author papers in internationally renowned journals, including PNAS,
Emotion, and the Journal of Experimental Social Psychology. She currently serves on the
Editorial Board of the Journal of Cultural Cognitive Science and has led research
projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (Young Scientist
Program) and the Hangzhou Municipal Natural Science Foundation (Young Scientist
Program).

This award reflects the high recognition of Dr. Fang Xia’s research contributions in the
fields of emotion science and cross-cultural psychology by the international academic
community, and also demonstrates the sustained development and growing international
influence of the Department of Psychology and Behavioral Sciences at Zhejiang University
in these research directions.
